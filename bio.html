Biography of Chandan

Chandan is a PhD scholar in the field of Embedded Systems Designing at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, where he is supervised by Professor Subrat Kar in the Electrical Department. He joined the program in 2021, bringing with him a wealth of knowledge and expertise in the field. He currently serves as a Senior Research Fellow (SRF) at the Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Delhi, and also holds the position of Additional Director at Suxma Systems Pvt. Ltd.

Prior to pursuing his PhD, Chandan completed his Master of Science in Electronics from the Department of Electronic Science at the University of Delhi, where he was a University gold medalist at his M.Sc. examination. He has a strong background in PCB designing and microcontroller programming, skills that have helped him excel in his studies and research.

Before his master's degree, Chandan completed a Bachelor of Science (Honors) in Electronics from Zakir Hussain Delhi College, University of Delhi. Throughout his academic career, Chandan has demonstrated a strong dedication to his studies and a passion for the field of electronics and embedded systems.

As a PhD scholar and SRF, Chandan is focusing his research on the design and implementation of embedded systems for various applications, including precision animal husbandry and structural vibration analysis. He is particularly interested in exploring the use of microcontrollers, machine learning, and IoT to create efficient and effective solutions for a range of real-world problems.

With his expertise in PCB design and microcontroller programming, Chandan is well-equipped to tackle the challenges of his research and make meaningful contributions to the field.

Introduction

Chandan is a PhD Scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, pursuing a degree in Embedded Systems Designing under the supervision of Prof. Subrat Kar. He joined the program in December 2021, and his research focuses on the analysis of health monitoring systems and estrus cycle detection in cows using embedded systems. Prior to his PhD, Chandan worked as an Embedded Engineer at Trydan Motors Pvt. Ltd., where he designed a Vehicle Control Unit for an electric bicycle from 2019 to 2021. In 2018–2020, he completed his Master's degree in Electronics from the Department of Electronic Science at the University of Delhi, where he was a University gold medalist at the M.Sc. examination.

Chandan has expertise in PCB designing and microcontroller programming, and holds a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Electronics from Zakir Husain Delhi College, University of Delhi, which he completed in 2015–2018.
